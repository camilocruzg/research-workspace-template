# Example concept

## Claim

*State the idea as a claim in two or three sentences: what you now think, not what the topic is. This section is the part new material should be able to change. When the weekly synthesis consolidates this page, it is this section that gets revised.*

## Where it came from

*Which work produced it: the project, the conversation, the problem that made the old framing fail. Link the project: [[example-project]].*

## Support

*Evidence, examples and sources, grouped by the point they make rather than one section per source. When several sections make the same point, merge them.*

## Open questions

*What would change the claim. What you have not yet tested.*
